Before you arrive

ICheck-in4 easy steps
  1. Show your Guest Access Form (sent to your Gmail) at the gate and lobby.
  2. Take the elevator to your floor.
  3. Open the key box at the door using the code we send you.
  4. Enjoy your stay!
IICheck-out4 quick steps
  1. Throw your trash in the bin at the end of the hallway.
  2. Turn off the aircon, lights and appliances.
  3. Put the keys back in the key box and lock the door.
  4. Send us a photo of the room.
IIIHouse rules6 rules for a cozy stay
  1. Please check in and check out on time.
  2. Maximum of 4 pax only — no extra guests allowed. Kids above 2 years old count as 1 pax.
  3. Every guest needs a valid ID.
  4. ₱1,000 security deposit — we give it back after check-out if everything is okay.
  5. No smoking or vaping inside the unit.
  6. No extra visitors — only the guests on your booking.
